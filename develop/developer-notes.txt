    Domain seed
        A moving character seeds only its destination cell, at (1 - progress) / speed; the origin is not seeded because the wave reaches it back from the destination. The duplicate check uses the direction the wave comes from, not the seed direction. The old seed spread from the origin in every direction, so a ghost moving away from a door still owned it. Effects: the circuit battery went from 9/9 escapes to 1/9 (cover 13% -> 15%), the trap still catches 20/20, and the blind ghost decisions went from 67% to 0%. The ghost domain is smaller while moving, so the territory weights may need rebalancing.
    Heuristic
        evaluateSearchState is SEARCH_VALUE_LIVES * (1 - lives/initialLives) + SEARCH_VALUE_CONTROL * (1 - control) + SEARCH_VALUE_DANGER * danger - SEARCH_VALUE_PROGRESS * progress - SEARCH_VALUE_PREY * prey, every term a fraction in [0,1]. progress is eaten/initial pellets; control is the share of the remaining pellets Pac-Man reaches before any ghost, weighted by 1/(1+wait); danger and prey are the closeness (1 - distance/radius, radius 4 cells) of the nearest ghost, split by whether it is frightened. The old formula was ((1 + Dg*Pg*Wg)/(1 + Dp*Pp*Wp) - k*(1 + totalPellets*lives)/(1 + remainingPellets*initialLives))/remainingPellets: the product was 0 whenever a domain had no power pellet (almost always, the map has four), so one move could jump the ratio from 0.005 to 533, and dividing by the remaining pellets moved the scale from 1e-4 to 1e-2 over a level, which the calibration buckets could not follow. The new value keeps one scale and makes a lost life cost LIVES/initialLives = 0.30, above the whole control range (0.25) and equal to the whole level of progress (0.30). Effects: Pac-Man stops hugging the ghosts (average distance to the nearest ghost 3 -> 5 cells over the 25 s simulation) without eating fewer pellets; the trap still catches 20/20 and the circuit battery goes from 4/9 to 1/9 escapes. pacman-avoid still fails its playout: the one-step value prefers the escape by a wide margin (0.27 against 0.65 for the death) but the 24-200 extension RBFM does not propagate a death two plies down. Calibration must be rerun (CALIBRATION_VERSION 6).
    Benchmark
        src/tests/strategy/bench.js plays complete seeded games with the strategy in process (no worker, no real time), one context per game, and reports wins, cleared fraction, deaths, time and pellets per second. --walks is the search budget per decision (the worker spends a similar number of walks between two decisions), --weights overrides keys of config.strategy, --jobs runs the games on several threads and --json prints one line. Baseline with the normalised value (20 games, 6 walks, 90 s, classic map): 0 wins, 0.59 cleared on average, 0.6 deaths, 1.15 pellets/s, ~14 s of CPU per game. Coordinate sweep over SEARCH_VALUE_PROGRESS, SEARCH_VALUE_DANGER and SEARCH_VALUE_DANGER_RADIUS (10 games per point, 4 jobs, then 20 games for the finalists): PROGRESS 1 clears 0.59 against 0.51 with 0.3, and 0.15 (0.39), 1.5 (0.57) and 2 (0.53) are worse, so it is set to 1. DANGER 0.3 gives the same games as 0.15, RADIUS 3 ties with 4 (0.57 both) and RADIUS 6 is worse (0.42); with ~0.05 standard error those are not separable. SEARCH_VALUE_DANGER 5 gives 0 deaths but only 0.23 cleared. CONTROL was swept later: 0.1 (0.58) and 0.5 (0.45) are worse than 0.25 (0.66), and PREY 0 is worse (0.58) while 0.4 ties, so both stay. LIVES between 0.6 and 1.5 gives identical games: the life term only differs when a death line is inside the explored tree. Search budget: 3 walks clear 0.44, 6 clear 0.66, 12 clear 0.57 and 24 clear 0.52 with deaths falling 0.60 -> 0.50 -> 0.40 -> 0.30, so more search makes Pac-Man safer but not better fed.
        The 90 s cap hides the endgame, so the level was never cleared. At 180 s (20 games) the baseline clears 0.65 with 0.75 deaths and still never clears a level. Pac-Man oscillates there: a 90 s game had 175 reversals in 316 decisions and the two moves had exactly the same value, so standing still is free rather than profitable. A permanently higher pellet attraction or a power pellet term made it worse (pellet 0.1/0.2/0.4 cleared 0.60/0.60/0.57; power 0.1/0.3 cleared 0.47/0.40; a defensive power term 0.3/0.6 cleared 0.63/0.66). The phase dependent pellet attraction works instead: SEARCH_VALUE_PELLET 0.2 weighted by the distance to the nearest pellet, only from the moment 35% of the level or less is left (SEARCH_VALUE_PELLET_PHASE), clears 0.67 with the same 0.75 deaths and gives the first cleared levels (2 wins in 30 games against 0 before).
